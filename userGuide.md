# User Guide — MMR Passport Renewal Monitoring

A free community tool for Myanmar citizens in Singapore. It watches the
Myanmar Embassy's passport approval/renewal lists for you and messages you on
Telegram the moment your name appears — so you don't have to keep checking
manually every week.

This is **not an official embassy service**. It is a volunteer-run tool built
to help the community. See the Terms & Conditions on the signup page for the
full disclaimer.

## How to sign up

1. Go to the website's home page.
2. Tap **Connect Telegram**. This opens a chat with our Telegram bot.
3. Tap **Start** in that chat. The bot replies instantly with your Telegram
   ID — tap it to copy.
4. Paste that ID into the **Your Telegram ID** field on the signup form.
5. Enter your **full name exactly as it appears on your passport** (it will
   be saved and shown in capital letters, to match how it appears on
   official documents).
6. Enter your **passport number**, if you have it. This is the most
   reliable way we have to match you against the embassy's list, so include
   it if you can.
7. Read the **Terms & Conditions** and check the box agreeing to them.
8. Tap **Notify me**.

You cannot submit the form without connecting Telegram and agreeing to the
Terms & Conditions — both are required so we can actually reach you and so
you understand how your information is used before you submit it.

After you submit, the bot sends you a one-time confirmation message on
Telegram, and you'll see a **status link**. Bookmark that link — it's the
only way to check your own status later without typing anything in again.

## If you already signed up before

Just fill in the form again with the same name/passport/Telegram ID. We
won't create a duplicate entry — we'll recognize you and show your existing
status link instead. This is also how you can add a missing Telegram ID to
an old entry, or correct a typo.

## Checking your status

Open your bookmarked status link at any time. It shows:

- Your name and passport number (as entered)
- Whether you're **Pending** (no match yet), have a **matched appointment
  date**, or are **Paused**
- When it was last updated

It does not show anyone else's information.

## What the alerts mean

On the public **Alerts** tab, you can see a feed of everyone the system has
successfully matched and notified — but names are partially hidden (e.g.
`KAUXX HTXX`) so you can recognize your own entry without seeing anyone
else's full name. Telegram IDs and passport numbers are also partially
masked everywhere on the public site.

## Your data

- We only use your name, passport number, and Telegram ID to match you
  against the embassy's publicly posted lists and to message you a result.
- We do not sell, share, or use your information for anyone's personal
  benefit — see the full Terms & Conditions on the signup page.
- Only submit your own information, or someone else's with their consent.
- This tool can make mistakes (OCR misreads, shared names, source-list
  changes) — always double-check with the embassy directly before making
  travel or other plans based on an alert here.

## Frequently asked questions

**I didn't get a Telegram message after signing up — what do I do?**
Make sure you tapped **Start** in the chat with the bot before submitting
the form, and that the Telegram ID you pasted matches exactly. If you're
still not sure, submit the form again with the same details — it will
recognize your existing entry and try again rather than creating a new one.

**I don't have my passport number yet — can I still sign up?**
Yes, but matching is far more reliable with it. Add it later by signing up
again with the same name and Telegram ID — your entry will be updated, not
duplicated.

**How often is the list checked?**
Every few hours, automatically, around the clock.

**Can I remove my information?**
Yes — send `/delete` to the bot on Telegram and your information is removed
immediately, no waiting required. You can also reach out to the project
directly if you'd rather not use the bot.

**Is this run by the embassy?**
No. This is an independent, volunteer, non-commercial community project. It
is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of the Myanmar
Embassy in Singapore or any government agency.
